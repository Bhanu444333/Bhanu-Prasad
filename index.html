01/About

Bridging the gap between graduation and career.

For nearly three years, I have specialized in mentoring final-year college students through one of the most defining moments of their lives: the campus-to-corporate transition.

Having delivered comprehensive communication, interview readiness, and group discussion training to ~1,000 students across 30+ batches, my approach combines rigorous behavioral drills with empathetic, confidence-building mentorship.

I believe every student possesses inherent strengths that deserve to be seen. Through deep one-on-one counseling and close coordination with partner colleges, I help learners overcome self-doubt, articulate their value with clarity, and walk into high-stakes interviews with calm conviction.

Recently, I have expanded my toolkit with digital marketing and modern productivity tools, enabling me to teach students not just classical interview etiquette, but also contemporary professional self-branding and tech fluency.
